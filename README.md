# 📘 Attrition Prevention Suggestions
 1. Enhance Employee Engagement
    
  Conduct regular surveys and act on feedback.
  Recognize employee achievements.
  Promote work-life balance.

3. Improve Career Development
   
  Provide clear career paths.
  Offer mentorship and coaching programs.
  Fund upskilling and training.

5. Optimize Compensation & Benefits
   
  Benchmark salaries to remain competitive.
  Offer performance bonuses and equity.
  Improve non-monetary benefits (e.g., healthcare, flexible hours).

7. Address Workload & OverTime

  Monitor employee workload to prevent burnout.
  Promote fair scheduling and limit excessive overtime.

9. Strengthen Manager-Employee Relationships
    
  Train managers in emotional intelligence and leadership.
  Encourage regular 1-on-1 check-ins.

11. Use Predictive Analytics
    
  Identify high-risk employees using ML models.
  Proactively intervene with targeted retention strategies.
